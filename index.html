About Me

I’m Kevin Nicol, a stagehand and multi-department deck assistant based in Reynoldsburg, Ohio. My background connects arena builds, lighting shop prep, location sound, camera work, and music for broadcast.

From Omaha Sets to Arena Floors

I began with commercial grip and production-assistant work in Omaha in 2003. Over 12 years with Bill and Ron Chvala, I grew into key grip, lead location audio recordist, and B-camera operator. Alongside that work, I spent five years as an I.A.T.S.E. Local 42 over-hire stagehand, working national arena tours, Broadway road shows, Olympic Swimming Trials builds, and trade shows.

At Theatrical Media Services, I learned the preparation behind those calls — checking fixtures, building cable packages, organizing road cases, and loading equipment so it was ready for the next crew.

Documentary Work in the Field

With Chvala & Company Productions, I worked on Opportunity Education expeditions through Uganda, Tanzania, and Zanzibar, plus a 20-day Evangelism Explosion shoot on Viti Levu, Fiji. I recorded dialogue, operated the boom, assisted with light and camera support, and kept equipment ready as locations and conditions changed.

Back in Omaha, the same approach carried into three years of multi-camera classroom production, documentary interviews, tabletop camera work, and commercial shoots.

On Call in Greater Columbus

Today I take W-2 stagehand calls with RockForce and freelance shifts through TechNicol Productions LLC. My Columbus work includes steel decks, stadium conversions, lighting, convention A/V, and dance and cheer flooring with Rhino Staging, AKAV LLC, and ON Services.

I report early, follow department leads, and stay focused through load-out. I carry OSHA 10 and CPR/AED and First Aid credentials, ANSI PPE, my tools, and $1M / $2M commercial general liability coverage for my independent 1099 work.

Music for Picture

I composed six original copyrighted scores for the nationally broadcast PBS documentary America’s Marine Aviators. My electronic album My Driving Silence was released in 2006. Recording and composing also gave me practical experience with signal flow, microphones, DAWs, and multitrack audio.
