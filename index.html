Union, Touring & Shop Work

May 2008 – August 2013 · Participant ID #422654

I.A.T.S.E. Local 42

As an Omaha union over-hire stagehand, I worked roughly 100 national arena tour calls, including Metallica, AC/DC, Foo Fighters, KISS, Slipknot/Knotfest, Taylor Swift, Celine Dion, Katy Perry, Tom Petty, Dave Matthews Band, Neil Diamond, Eagles, Fleetwood Mac, Billy Joel & Elton John, and Lil Wayne. Assignments included staging, truss and lighting support, equipment moves, strike, and packing for load-out.

At the Omaha Orpheum Theatre, I worked on Broadway and opera calls for Wicked, Jersey Boys, Avenue Q, Beauty & The Beast, La Bohème, and Pagliacci. During the 2008 and 2012 U.S. Olympic Swimming Trials, I helped assemble two regulation competition pools brought in from Italy for the arena builds.

Additional union calls included Disney On Ice, WWE SmackDown, the NCAA College World Series, and Freeman Decorating / Freeman A/V expos and STRATCOM Defense Symposiums. These involved moving freight onto show floors, assembling exhibit and scenic elements, and working load-out as venues changed over.

2008–2013 · Omaha & Council Bluffs

Theatrical Media Services (T.M.S.) & Trans-Siberian Orchestra (T.S.O.)

At T.M.S., I handled warehouse logistics and L2 shop prep. The work included bench testing Martin MAC automated luminaires and 48-channel dimmer racks, prepping 400A Cam-Lok distribution, grandMA and Hog consoles, CM Lodestar chain hoists, 12- and 20-inch box truss, and Socapex and DMX looms. I staged equipment by department and organized case placement for efficient 53-foot semi-trailer packs.

In October 2008, 2009, and 2011, I worked multi-week T.S.O. technical rehearsals for the East and West Coast winter tours at the Mid-America Center. I supported the installation and changeovers for arena steel, moving-light trusses, over-audience catwalks, more than 30 stage elevators, and three-phase power distribution. The crews coordinated around pyro and laser safety perimeters while both touring packages rehearsed.

Documentary, Broadcast & Commercial Production

April 2003 – August 2015 · Omaha & International

Chvala & Company Productions & Early Commercial Grip Work

For 12 years I worked under Bill and Ron Chvala as key grip, lead location audio recordist, B-camera operator, and third in command. My responsibilities ranged from shaping light, rigging and placing camera support, and recording dialogue and ambient sound to stepping onto B-camera and keeping field equipment moving between locations.

For Opportunity Education Foundation, I worked on expeditions in Uganda, Tanzania, and Zanzibar. In Dar es Salaam, the crew documented state meetings involving Tanzanian President Jakaya Kikwete and Opportunity Education founder Joe Ricketts. In Omaha I helped produce three years of the multi-camera classroom studio series, setting up camera support, lighting, and location audio. I also worked the foundation’s 2013 gala featuring Kelly Clarkson.

The documentary work included a 20-day Evangelism Explosion expedition in Fiji, the Best of Show Boys Town Centennial documentary, and productions for Mosaic Healthcare. For the PBS documentary America’s Marine Aviators I composed six original music cues. IMDb

My early commercial work (2003–2006) included grip and production assistant calls on Omaha shoots for Nebraska State Bank, Huber Chevrolet/Cadillac, and Metro Community College. Those smaller sets gave me practice setting up lighting and camera support quickly and coordinating equipment moves between setups.
