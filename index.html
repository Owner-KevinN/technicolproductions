Our Capabilities

Comprehensive technical production services engineered for event planners, agencies, and venues requiring dependable execution.

TechNicol Productions LLC is an Ohio-based technical services company, owned and operated by Lead Technician and Stagehand, Kevin Nicol.

We offer professional audio/visual engineering support, freelance stagehand labor, Lighting Assist (L2), Audio Assist (A2), Video Assist (V2), set and strike capabilities in all departments, power distribution, and versatile venue operations of all sorts.

We specialize in contract production services for corporate conferences, conventions, arena shows, and live entertainment venues.

Our mission is to deliver reliable, high-precision technical support, signal flow management, and stage operations for event planners, production agencies, and venues across the Greater Columbus area and beyond.

Multi-Department Stagecraft

Arena deck builds, structural staging, pipe & drape, custom springboard performance flooring, and high-speed truck loading.

Lighting Assist (L2 / LX Tech)

Audio Assist (A2)

Video Assist (V2) & Breakout AV

Field Notes & Technical Dispatch

Engineering principles, technical workflows, and operational methodologies developed across 15+ years on stage decks and field shoots.

1. Audio Signal Flow & RF Management

  • Strict gain-staging discipline from microphone preamps through DSP to power amplifiers.
  • Wireless RF spectrum scanning and intermodulation calculation to guarantee drop-out free audio in dense convention centers.
  • Digital console multi-bus routing for independent broadcast, record, and house sound mixes.

2. Power Distro & Electrical Safety

  • Calculating load balances across 3-phase Cam-Lok feeder legs to prevent neutral overload.
  • Testing grounding integrity and ground-fault protection on outdoor stadium stages.
  • Isolation protocols for high-draw automated moving lights and specialized stage effects.

3. TSO's Custom-Built Designs & Technical Heritage

  • Design & Technical Heritage: The revolutionary staging innovations dreamt up by Trans-Siberian Orchestra founder Paul O’Neill and mapped out, designed, and custom-built by Lighting Designer Bryan Hartley.
  • Custom Staging Elements: Assembly, calibration, and mechanics of automated moving trusses, the famous Over-Audience Catwalks, 30+ Stage Elevators, the Fire Screen, and high-voltage Tesla Coils.
  • Dual-Tour Rehearsal Challenge: The unique technical challenge of setting up, bench-testing, and synchronizing two identical, state-of-the-art touring productions simultaneously for East and West Coast launches at the Mid-America Center.

4. International Logistics & Off-Grid Ops

  • Maintaining 24/7 battery charging and solar-supported field setups in Tanzania, Zanzibar, and Uganda.
  • Managing Carnet customs documentation and delicate broadcast gear transport across international borders.
  • Executing remote foot deliveries of educational technology to off-grid rural schools.
